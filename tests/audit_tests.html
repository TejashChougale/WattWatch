Privacy & Data Architecture Notice

Browser LocalStorage Persistence WattWatch operates primarily as a client-side application. Your appliance inventory, tariff parameters, monthly budgets, and historical snapshots are stored locally in your browser's localStorage under dedicated workspace namespaces.
Guest Mode Guest appliance and billing data stays in browser localStorage and is not uploaded to WattWatch's cloud database, while external resources (such as web fonts and library scripts) may still load. No account credentials are required.
Firebase Authentication WattWatch does not ask users to provide a personal email address or phone number. Account authentication uses a generated username-based identifier.
Cloud Firestore Profile Storage Remote cloud storage is strictly limited to your basic user profile record (/users/{uid}) containing username and display name. Your household appliances and bills remain exclusively in browser localStorage.
Google External Requests External network requests are made solely to Google Firebase endpoints (Authentication & Firestore) and static CDNs (Google Fonts, Chart.js fallback). No analytics trackers or third-party ad networks are used.
Important Privacy & Portability Limitations:
  • No Cross-Device Sync: Appliances and snapshots are saved in local browser storage and do not automatically sync across different browsers or devices.
  • No Automated Password Recovery: Because WattWatch collects neither email addresses nor telephone numbers, automated password reset emails cannot be delivered.
